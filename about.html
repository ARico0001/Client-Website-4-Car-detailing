The Story

Hi, I'm Alex Smith.

I am a visual storyteller specializing in street, lifestyle, and atmospheric landscape photography. Based out of California, I spend my time chasing natural light and raw human moments.

My obsession with the frozen frame began over a decade ago with a dusty analog film camera. Today, that passion has evolved into a dedicated pursuit of capturing the honest, unscripted segments of daily life. I believe the best photographs aren't manufactured—they're found.
